01 · What is GMCThe city, in one breath
Brief · Sacred ↔ StrategicThe opening uses the serif voice (Newsreader) for the vision, and the words light up as you read. Plain facts and dated numbers follow straight after, so investors get substance fast.

Gelephu Mindfulness City is a new city in the foothills of the Himalaya, where Bhutan opens to the world and prosperity is measured by wellbeing as well as wealth.

i.

A Special Administrative Region

GMC has its own laws and regulators, built for international business, within the Kingdom of Bhutan.

ii.

Guided by Gross National Happiness

Growth is measured by wellbeing, culture and the health of the land, not by GDP alone.

iii.

A city built with nature

Forests, farmland and rivers like the Mau Chhu shape the plan. The city grows around them, not over them.
